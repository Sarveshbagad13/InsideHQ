# NovaSphere Organization Structure
The company is led by the Chief Executive Officer (CEO). Department heads report to the CEO.

Engineering is led by the VP of Engineering and is divided into Platform, Product Engineering, QA, DevOps, and SRE.

Product is led by the Chief Product Officer (CPO). Product Managers work with Engineering and Design.

Customer-facing teams include Sales, Customer Success, and Support.

Corporate functions include Finance, Human Resources, Legal, and Information Security.

The Engineering Platform team owns shared authentication, APIs, databases, and internal developer tooling. Product Engineering owns FlowOps, InsightHub, and SecureDesk features.
