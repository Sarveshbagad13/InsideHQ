# Work From Home Policy
Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

Employees must remain reachable during core collaboration hours from 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM IST.

Remote work requires a reliable internet connection and a secure working environment.

Confidential company information must not be viewed on shared or public computers.

Employees should use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from untrusted networks.

Long-term remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.
