# Customer Support SLA
Support priority is determined by customer impact and urgency.

P1: widespread or critical service impact. Initial response target: 30 minutes.

P2: significant functionality affected with a workaround unavailable or inadequate. Initial response target: 4 business hours.

P3: normal product issue or question. Initial response target: 1 business day.

SLA targets may differ for Enterprise customers according to their contract.
