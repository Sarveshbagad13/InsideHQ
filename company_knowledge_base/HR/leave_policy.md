# Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 24 paid leave days per calendar year:
- 18 annual/privilege leave days
- 6 sick leave days

Employees should request planned leave at least three working days in advance through the HR portal.

For unplanned sick leave, employees should notify their manager as soon as practical.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year.

Leave beyond available balances requires manager and HR approval.

Public holidays are separate from the annual leave allowance.
