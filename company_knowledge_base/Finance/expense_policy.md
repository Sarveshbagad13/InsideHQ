# Expense Policy
Employees may claim reasonable business expenses that are necessary for company work.

Receipts are required for expenses above INR 1,000.

Expenses should normally be submitted within 30 days.

Business travel must be approved before booking whenever practical.

Meals during business travel are reimbursable within the applicable daily allowance.

Personal expenses, fines, entertainment without business justification, and unauthorized upgrades are not reimbursable.
