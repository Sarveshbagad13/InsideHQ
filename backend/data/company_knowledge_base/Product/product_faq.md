# Product FAQ
### Can FlowOps integrate with external systems?
Yes. FlowOps supports API-based integrations and selected prebuilt connectors.

### Does InsightHub support scheduled reports?
Yes. Users with the appropriate permissions can configure scheduled reports.

### Can SecureDesk manage internal IT requests?
Yes. SecureDesk supports service requests, incidents, assignments, and escalation workflows.

### Is multi-factor authentication supported?
Yes. MFA is supported for company accounts and can be enforced by administrators for supported plans.
