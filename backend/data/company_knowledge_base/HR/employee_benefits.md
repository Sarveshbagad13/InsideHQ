# Employee Benefits
Eligible full-time employees receive medical insurance coverage according to the company insurance plan.

Employees may claim approved business expenses under the Finance Expense Policy.

The company provides a learning allowance of INR 20,000 per financial year for approved courses, certifications, conferences, and technical books.

Employees must obtain manager approval before making learning-related purchases above INR 5,000.

Benefits are subject to the terms of the applicable company plans and policies.
