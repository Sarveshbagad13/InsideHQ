# Product Pricing
NovaSphere offers Starter, Business, and Enterprise plans.

Starter is intended for small teams and has limited automation and analytics capacity.

Business includes advanced workflow automation, expanded reporting, and priority support.

Enterprise provides higher limits, advanced security controls, custom integrations, and contractual support terms.

Exact enterprise pricing is negotiated based on users, usage, integrations, support requirements, and contract terms. Sales must provide the official quote.
