# Engineering Incident Management
Incidents are classified as P1, P2, or P3.

P1 incidents represent major production impact or widespread customer impact. The on-call engineer must immediately engage the incident response process.

P2 incidents have significant but limited impact.

P3 incidents are lower-impact issues that can normally be handled through standard engineering workflows.

For P1 incidents, the incident commander coordinates technical response and communication.

After a significant incident, the team performs a blameless post-incident review and records corrective actions.
