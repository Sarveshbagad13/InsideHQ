# Department Responsibilities
## Engineering
Builds, tests, deploys, and maintains company software.

## Product
Defines product strategy, requirements, priorities, and roadmap.

## Customer Success
Owns onboarding, adoption, account health, and customer relationships after sale.

## Support
Handles technical support tickets, troubleshooting, and incident escalation.

## Sales
Owns lead qualification, proposals, negotiations, and new customer acquisition.

## Finance
Handles budgeting, payments, expenses, reimbursements, procurement, and financial reporting.

## Human Resources
Handles hiring, employee records, leave, benefits, performance processes, and workplace policies.

## Information Security
Owns security policies, access reviews, incident response, vulnerability management, and security awareness.
