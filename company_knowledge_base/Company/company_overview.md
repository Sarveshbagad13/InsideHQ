# NovaSphere Technologies — Company Overview
## About
NovaSphere Technologies is a fictional B2B SaaS company that provides workflow automation and analytics software for mid-sized businesses. The company headquarters is in Pune, India, with remote employees across India and Singapore.

## Products
NovaSphere has three primary products:
- FlowOps: workflow automation platform.
- InsightHub: business analytics and reporting platform.
- SecureDesk: internal service and support management platform.

## Departments
The major departments are Engineering, Product, Sales, Customer Success, Support, Finance, Human Resources, and Information Security.

## Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST. Engineering teams may use flexible schedules when approved by their manager.

## Company Values
Customer focus, ownership, transparency, continuous learning, security by design, and measurable impact.
