# Employee Handbook
Employees are expected to act professionally, protect company information, follow security policies, and communicate respectfully.

Employees receive access to systems based on job responsibilities. Sharing credentials is prohibited.

The standard probation period is six months for new full-time employees. Performance feedback is provided during probation and during the annual performance cycle.

Employees must report conflicts of interest to Human Resources.

Company equipment remains company property and must be returned when employment ends.

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a secure workspace and should avoid accessing confidential information on public computers.
