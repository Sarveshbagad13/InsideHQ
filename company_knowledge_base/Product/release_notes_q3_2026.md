# Q3 2026 Release Notes
FlowOps 4.8 introduced improved workflow retry handling and additional webhook diagnostics.

InsightHub 3.2 added dashboard sharing controls and scheduled CSV exports.

SecureDesk 2.6 introduced configurable escalation timers.

A known limitation in FlowOps 4.8 is that certain legacy webhook integrations may display incomplete diagnostic metadata. Engineering is working on a follow-up patch.
